# Joel Chadabe — *Electric Sound: The Past and Promise of Electronic Music*

Prentice Hall, 1997.

## Seen in university teaching

- Ohio University, MUS 4130/5130, *The History and Practice of Electronic Music*: strongly suggested text; chapters 1–9 are assigned selectively across the semester.
- UC San Diego, Music 6, *Electronic Music* (Fall 2006): the chapter “Synthesizers,” pp. 140–171, is assigned for the synthesizer unit.
