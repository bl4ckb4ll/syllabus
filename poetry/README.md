# Poetry

Poetry, performance, and listening material in the syllabus repository.

## Texts

- [Jack London — *Ode to a Scab*](Jack%20London%20%E2%80%93%20Ode%20to%20a%20Scab.md)
- [Robert Frost — *Two Tramps in Mud Time*](Robert%20Frost%20%E2%80%93%20Two%20Tramps%20in%20Mud%20Time.md) — work for pleasure versus work for pay; class, skill, ownership, economic need, vocation and avocation; directly documented in upper-division and graduate university courses.
- [Robert Frost — *The Death of the Hired Man*](Robert%20Frost%20%E2%80%93%20The%20Death%20of%20the%20Hired%20Man.md) — aging and seasonal farm labor, dignity, dependence, employer obligation, practical knowledge and formal education; directly documented at MIT and UT Dallas.

## Discovery and indexing

- [Poetry Foundation — topics, emotions, and public-domain corpus](poetry-foundation/README.md) — preserves the useful many-to-many topic/emotion indexing idea while keeping copyrighted Foundation material as links/summaries and sourcing public-domain text independently.
- [Emotion classifier index](emotions/README.md) — stable emotion-label directories containing symbolic links to canonical works; intended to become a reusable training/classification corpus.

## Audio archives

- [PennSound — poetry audio archive](PennSound%20%E2%80%93%20poetry%20audio%20archive.md) — historical author recordings, readings, talks, radio, sound poetry, multilingual material, and performance archives from the University of Pennsylvania.
