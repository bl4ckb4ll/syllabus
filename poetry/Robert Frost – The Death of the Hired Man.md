# The Death of the Hired Man

- **Author:** Robert Frost
- **First published:** 1914
- **Type:** Poem
- **University appearances:** 2+
- **Notable courses:** MIT — 21L.004 Reading Poetry (Spring 2018); University of Texas at Dallas — HUSL 6312 Frost and Stevens (Spring 2015)
- **Typically taught:** Undergraduate and graduate
- **Format:** Full poem
- **Context:** Narrative poetry; farm labor; seasonal employment; aging; dignity; dependence; employer obligation; practical knowledge and formal education; definitions of home.

## Publication note

The poem appears in Frost's *North of Boston*, first published in London by David Nutt in **1914**. A 1915 Henry Holt U.S. edition is also freely available in public-domain scans.
