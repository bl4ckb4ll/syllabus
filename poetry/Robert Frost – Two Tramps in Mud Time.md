# Two Tramps in Mud Time

- **Author:** Robert Frost
- **First published:** 1934
- **Type:** Poem
- **University appearances:** 4+
- **Notable courses:** College of Charleston — ENGL 335 Modern Poetry (Spring 2014); San José State University — ENGL 211 Graduate Seminar in Twentieth-Century Poetry (Fall 2013; Fall 2016); University of Texas at Dallas — HUSL 6312 Frost and Stevens (Spring 2015)
- **Typically taught:** Upper-division undergraduate and graduate
- **Format:** Full poem
- **Context:** Modern poetry; manual labor; class; vocation and avocation; skill; ownership; economic need; work chosen for pleasure versus work sought for pay.

## Publication note

Dartmouth's Robert Frost collection and Amherst College's Frost periodicals inventory both record the poem in *The Saturday Review of Literature* on **October 6, 1934**. It was later collected in *A Further Range* (1936). Some teaching schedules date it 1936 because they organize it under that collection.
