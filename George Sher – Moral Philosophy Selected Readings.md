# George Sher (editor) — *Moral Philosophy: Selected Readings*

## FSU evidence

Florida State University PHI 3670, **Ethical Theory**, taught by David McNaughton, assigns this anthology.

FSU syllabus:
https://philosophy.fsu.edu/sites/g/files/imported/storage/original/application/9069e23b480dab77c016dcf0d4789cae.pdf

The surviving indexed syllabus identifies:

- George Sher, editor
- *Moral Philosophy: Selected Readings*
- second edition
- Wadsworth, 2001

Bibliographic records for ISBN `9780155017559` describe the second edition under the earlier Harcourt Brace imprint/date. The FSU syllabus is the authority here for what its course says it assigned; the discrepancy is recorded rather than silently normalized.

Open Library:
https://openlibrary.org/books/OL7364475M/Moral_Philosophy

Open Library marks the book **Preview Only**, not a freely downloadable full copy.

## What PHI 3670 actually assigns

Required anthology selections, in course order:

`2, 3, 4, 23, 26, 27, 43, 44, 29, 30, 28, 33, 35 (pp. 476–485), 40, 14, 8, 9, 10, 11, 19, 17, 18`

Selections `31` and `32` are listed as supplementary rather than required.

The schedule also assigns two Blackboard readings, one by Adams and one by Frankena. Their exact titles have not yet been recovered, so they are not guessed here.
