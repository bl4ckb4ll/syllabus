# Tara Rodgers — *Pink Noises: Women on Electronic Music and Sound*

Duke University Press, 2010.

## Seen in university teaching

- Johns Hopkins / Peabody, PY.610.650, *Pink Noise: Women Making Electronic Music*: the course description says the seminar is inspired and guided by Rodgers’s anthology.

The course uses the book to frame feminist analysis of electronic music alongside Deep Listening and the work of Daphne Oram, Pauline Oliveros, Wendy Carlos, Ruth Anderson, Adrian Piper, and contemporary artists.
