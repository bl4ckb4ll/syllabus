# Later Clemente reading corpus — Worcester Emergency Library, 2020

**LATER CLEMENTE — not the original 1995–96 syllabus.**

In May 2020, Jim Cocola, Academic Director of Clemente Worcester, assembled a **Clemente Emergency Library** of titles relevant to Clemente students and instructors. It is useful evidence for the later intellectual corpus around Clemente, but it must not be back-projected onto Earl Shorris's first class.

Source:
https://users.wpi.edu/~jcocola/clemente/index.html

The page was built around the Internet Archive's temporary National Emergency Library conditions in 2020. Access conditions have changed; the presence of an Archive.org link in that 2020 page is **not** a claim that every copyrighted book remains freely borrowable or downloadable today.

## Why keep this file

The original Clemente course asked a narrow historical question: what did Shorris and the first faculty actually teach in 1995–96? This later library answers a different and useful question: **what books did Clemente educators later regard as belonging to the intellectual world of the program?** Keeping the two corpora separate lets the repository answer both without falsifying either.
