# What would I read at Florida State University?

In David McNaughton’s Ethical Theory course, you would work through selections from George Sher’s anthology, including Mill on utilitarianism, Kant on moral reasoning, Nagel on moral luck, and Plato’s *Euthyphro*. For his Fall 2006 Philosophy of Religion course, the anthology is known and the individual selections are still missing.

## How much of the philosophy major does this cover?

FSU lists Ethical Theory as a major requirement. To answer for a full philosophy major we still need course readings for logic, ancient philosophy, Modern Philosophy, a metaphysics or epistemology course, the senior seminar, and electives. The evidence above covers two historical offerings. Contemporary sections need their own syllabi.
