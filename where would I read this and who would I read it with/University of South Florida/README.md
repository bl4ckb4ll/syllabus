# What would I read at the University of South Florida?

In the Fall 2026 sections documented here, you would read Foucault and Deleuze in Continental Philosophy III, ancient authors alongside modern historians in Classical Greece, and *Let’s Talk* with *The Little Seagull Handbook* in Composition I.

## How much of USF does this cover?

These are three published sections, one each in philosophy, history, and English. Each has its own reading list. The records do not establish what other sections or an entire major assign. One separate fragment from Education, EDG 7067, names Kerry E. Howell’s *An Introduction to the Philosophy of Methodology* as required.
