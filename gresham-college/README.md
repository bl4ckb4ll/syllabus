# Gresham College lecture index

Gresham College is indexed here as what it says it is: a free public-lecture institution, not a degree program. It sets no exams, awards no qualifications, and has no students. The archive is useful as an open lecture library and as a route from topics and speakers to further reading; it is not evidence of a curriculum completed by enrolled students.

## Main indexes

- [Watch Now](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now) — recent, recommended, popular, and featured lectures.
- [Browse All](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now/browse-all) — the main lecture browser.
- [Series](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now/series) — multi-lecture sequences and annual lectures.
- [Speakers](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/speakers) — Gresham Professors, Visiting Professors, and other lecturers.
- [Podcast episode index](https://podcast.gresham.ac.uk/1904814/episodes) — audio releases; the page reported 3,020 episodes on 2026-09-03.
- [Schools and Colleges](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/schools-and-colleges) — subject and school-oriented playlists.
- [About Gresham College](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/about-us) — institutional history, mission, and governance.

## Filing an individual lecture

When a lecture is used in a reconstructed course or reading path, inspect its own Gresham page and record:

- exact title;
- lecturer;
- lecture date, separately from podcast publication date;
- professorship or other role at the time;
- series and Gresham topic tags;
- lecture-page URL;
- available text, transcript, audio, video, downloads, and extra reading;
- the books, papers, primary sources, or other works discussed.

A lecture about a book or subject does not replace the underlying reading. Link the lecture to canonical books and papers already represented elsewhere in the repository.

## Completeness boundary

This is a hand-maintained navigation index over a changing external archive, not a claim that every Gresham lecture has been copied into this repository. On 2026-09-03 Gresham described its website as containing more than 3,500 videos, while the podcast episode page reported 3,020 episodes. Those are different publication surfaces and should not be silently treated as identical inventories.
