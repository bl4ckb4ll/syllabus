# Luigi Russolo — *The Art of Noises*

1913 futurist manifesto.

## Seen in university teaching

- UC San Diego, Music 6 (Fall 2006): assigned as the first reading in the course’s opening unit on music, sound, and modernism.
- Brown’s 2026–27 bulletin lists Russolo among the authors in MUSC 0021H, *Manifestos: Art, Politics and the Idea of Progress*; that catalog entry does not identify the exact Russolo text, so this second connection is not evidence that *The Art of Noises* itself is assigned there.
