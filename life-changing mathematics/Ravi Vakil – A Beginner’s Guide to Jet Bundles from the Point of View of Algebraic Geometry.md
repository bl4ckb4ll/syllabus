## Why this is here

This is a compact example of exposition that starts from a concrete enumerative question and builds just enough machinery to make the calculation feel inevitable. Instead of introducing jets as an isolated formal construction, the note explains them as a way to package the value of a section together with derivative information so that singularity conditions become vanishing conditions for a bundle section.

That route is especially useful for a reader who has seen Chern classes and vector bundles but has not yet developed an intuitive reason to care about jet bundles.

## Summary

The note gives a functional introduction to jet bundles and flags of jet bundles from the perspective of enumerative algebraic geometry. A guiding example asks how singular members occur in a general pencil of plane curves. Locally, the value and first derivatives of a section are assembled into first-order jet data; globally, that data is encoded by the first jet bundle. Vanishing of the induced jet section detects a singular point, and Chern-class calculations then turn the geometric condition into an enumerative count.

The note proceeds in the same spirit through higher jets and related constructions, with many exercises and an emphasis on calculations rather than a comprehensive literature survey.
