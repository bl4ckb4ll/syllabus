## Why this is here

Exact sequences are usually taught as strings of objects and arrows plus diagram chases. Vakil instead externalizes a mathematician’s private schematic intuition: subobjects, quotients, kernels, cokernels, and exactness become pieces of a visual calculus. That makes the note valuable not merely as another account of the definitions but as an example of how an expert actually pictures the subject while thinking.

The 3Blue1Brown introduction is explicit that the intended audience already has some familiarity with exact sequences, the Snake Lemma, cohomology, and related material. The point is not elementary introduction; it is a visual companion to the conventional algebra.
