# Life-changing mathematics

This directory is for mathematics papers, books, notes, retrospectives, and bibliographic collections that are worth preserving as paths into mathematics rather than merely recording as citations.

It is deliberately personal and non-canonical. Inclusion means that the work is unusually illuminating, beautiful, formative, generous, or capable of changing how a reader thinks about mathematics.

## House style

The model is the Armin Straub *What Is...?* continuity mirror: preserve good links and provenance, give credit generously, and add useful local commentary without pretending that our commentary is part of the original work.

For an individual paper or book, entries should normally contain:

1. **Links to the work.** Prefer the author’s page, publisher or journal page, DOI, arXiv/HAL record, and a lawful free copy when one exists.
2. **Thanks and credit.** Say explicitly when an author, editor, institution, or maintainer has made a useful copy, index, correction, or bibliography available. Preserve their role rather than silently absorbing their work into this repository.
3. **Why it is here.** Give a short, specific statement of what is excellent or unusual about the work. Praise should say *what the work does well*, not merely call it important.
4. **Our summary.** Write an original summary of the mathematical content and the route the paper or book takes through it. Do not copy an abstract and call that a summary.
5. **Sources cited by the work.** When practical, record the paper’s own important references, especially the sources on which its central constructions, historical claims, or arguments depend. Keep this separate from later papers that cite the work.
6. **Corrections, sequels, and retrospective commentary.** Link errata and later explanations when they materially change how the work should be read.
7. **Rights/provenance.** A freely downloadable PDF is not automatically licensed for republication. Default to links. Rehost complete text only when the public-domain status or redistribution license is explicit enough to justify doing so, and retain the license/attribution notice.
