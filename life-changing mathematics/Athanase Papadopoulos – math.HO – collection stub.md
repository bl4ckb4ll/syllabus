# Athanase Papadopoulos — arXiv `math.HO`

Status: **reconciled and complete as of 2026-08-31 for the stated scope**. Target scope is **every arXiv record confidently attributable to Athanase Papadopoulos carrying `math.HO` among its subjects**. The reconciled list contains **47 records**.

The broad arXiv author search for `Papadopoulos, A` is not treated as an identity oracle: it mixes Athanase Papadopoulos with several other authors whose given name begins with A. A candidate is promoted into the verified list only when the arXiv record identifies Athanase Papadopoulos (directly or through an exact title/coauthor match) **and** `math.HO` is present in the arXiv subject metadata. HAL is used as the independent bibliography/identity backstop.

## Deliberate exclusions and unresolved author matches

This collection is category-defined, not topic-defined. Historically oriented papers are excluded when arXiv does not actually carry `math.HO`. Examples checked during reconciliation include:

- [arXiv:2607.29263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29263), *On the construction of geographical maps: Lagrange, Chebyshev, Darboux and Milnor* — `math.DG`, `math.GT`, `math.MG`, not `math.HO`.
- [arXiv:2603.29444](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29444), *Notes on angles and solid angles, in relation with Euler's memoir De mensura angulorum solidorum* — `math.GT`, not `math.HO`.
- [arXiv:1409.4742](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.4742), *On hyperbolic analogues of some classical theorems in spherical geometry* — `math.MG`, `math.GT`, not `math.HO`.
- [arXiv:1406.7309](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.7309), *On Klein's So-called Non-Euclidean geometry* — `math.MG`, `math.GT`, not `math.HO`.
- [arXiv:1603.05378](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05378), *A Commentary on Teichmüller's paper "Vollständige Lösung einer Extremalaufgabe der quasikonformen Abbildung"* — `math.GT`, not `math.HO`.

HAL also contains Papadopoulos publications/preprints for which no corresponding arXiv record was recovered. Those are outside this arXiv-defined collection rather than silently treated as missing arXiv records.

Finally, any old search hit that identifies an author only as **A. Papadopoulos** remains a candidate, not an attribution, unless exact title/coauthor evidence reconciles it to the HAL bibliography. Ambiguous initial-only matches are deliberately left unresolved rather than guessed and are not counted among the 47 verified records.
