# Juanita Pinzón-Caicedo and Daniel Ruberman — *Applications of Instanton Floer Homology*

A survey article in the *Notices of the American Mathematical Society*, September 2022, volume 69, number 8, pages 1307–1319.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/noti2536
- AMS PDF: https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/202208/rnoti-p1307.pdf

The article surveys applications of instanton Floer homology, emphasizing areas of low-dimensional topology that had seen substantial recent activity.
