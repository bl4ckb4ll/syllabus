# Joshua Evan Greene — *Heegaard Floer Homology*

A survey article in the *Notices of the American Mathematical Society*, January 2021, volume 68, number 1, pages 19–33.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/noti2194
- AMS Notices feature listing: https://www.ams.org/cgi-bin/notices/nxgnotices.pl?cnt=feature&fm=gen
- Author page: https://sites.google.com/bc.edu/joshua-e-greene/

Greene describes this as a survey of Heegaard Floer homology: its development, evolution, applications, and possible future directions. His author page notes that the article received the 2023 AMS Levi L. Conant Prize.
